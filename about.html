ABOUT SAFETY EXPERTS

Dedicated to ensuring safer workplaces across various industries with quality PPE and industrial safety solutions.

Reliable & Trusted Supplier

Safety Experts, Delhi-06 is a reliable and trusted supplier of industrial safety products and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), dedicated to ensuring safer workplaces across various industries. Located in the well-known industrial hub of Ajmeri Gate, New Delhi, we have built a strong reputation for delivering quality products, prompt service, and customer satisfaction.

We specialize in a comprehensive range of PPE products, including safety helmets, safety shoes, hand gloves, masks, goggles, face shields, and protective wear. In addition, we offer a wide variety of traffic safety products such as safety cones, barricades, caution tapes, road studs, and reflective safety accessories, along with essential industrial safety gear.

Our products are widely used across industries such as construction and infrastructure, manufacturing units, road and traffic management projects, warehousing and logistics, and facility management services. With our manufacturing unit in Bawana Industrial Area, we maintain strict quality control and large inventories to meet bulk demands.
